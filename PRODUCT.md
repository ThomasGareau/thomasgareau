# Product

## Register
brand

## Users and purpose
Thomas Gareau-Paquette's bilingual academic website presents his political science research, publications, conference presentations, software and teaching. Readers include fellow researchers, prospective collaborators, students and people seeking his CV. Existing homepage and CV establish Cornell Government affiliation and research on political cleavages, welfare states, territorial identities and housing.

## Personality and anti-references
Personal, scholarly, specific. The owner requests a less generic site that does not look like an AI-generated template. Preserve academic titles, citations, portrait and direct access to papers. Avoid marketing claims, decorative dashboards, generic card grids and artificial illustrations.

## Principles
Make research easy to read and cite. Give English and French equal treatment. Ground all biographical changes in the supplied CV. Use typography and composition to distinguish the site rather than decorative effects. Keep static pages and direct download links dependable.

## Accessibility
Maintain readable contrast, keyboard focus, sensible heading order and layouts that work on narrow screens. Avoid motion-dependent content and external font dependencies.
