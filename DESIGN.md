# Research dossier

Light reading surface for colleagues browsing papers at a desk: cool grey-blue paper, dark teal ink, a restrained oxide-red accent. The reference is the cover and inside pages of a university working-paper series, with a typographic nameplate and a photographic author index.

Typography uses the platform Palatino family for the nameplate and headings, Avenir/Segoe UI for readable text and metadata. No remote font requests. The original portrait is retained in a rectangular crop. Desktop uses an author column and a wide reading column; on phones the author details follow the content. Navigation wraps instead of hiding behind a menu. Publication lists use fine horizontal rules and plain links, not cards. Numbered section labels give long bibliographies a clear index.

CSS tokens live in _sass/_custom.scss. Both languages share these tokens and layouts. Active links, keyboard focus and a skip link are explicit. Content has no animation dependency. Keep PRODUCT.md and DESIGN.md excluded from published output.
